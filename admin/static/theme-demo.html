Button family — light surface

Not a recolour: fills darken, text inverts to white, and the bloom becomes a focus ring because a halo on a light ground reads as a smudge. Gold is the exception in both directions — it keeps its bright fill and dark ink, because no gold light enough to look gold can carry white text.
