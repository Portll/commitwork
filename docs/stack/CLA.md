# Contributor Licence Agreement

The agreement in force is no longer drafted here. commitwork's contributor licence agreement is
[.github/CLA.md](../../.github/CLA.md), version 1 of 7 October 2026: the same agreement cobolwork
and ironwork use, adapted to commitwork's licences (AGPL-3.0-or-later, PolyForm Internal Use 1.0.0
by written agreement, and Apache-2.0 over the wire layer). It is a licence, not an assignment.

It replaces the September draft that stood here, which named a company not yet formed as the
grantee. The grantee is John Hancock trading as Portll, and section 9 passes the agreement to a
company incorporated to carry on the business without contributors being asked again.

How to sign is in [.github/CONTRIBUTING.md](../../.github/CONTRIBUTING.md).
